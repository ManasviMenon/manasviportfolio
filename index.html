Regime-Shift VaR Calibration (Multi-Task Transformer)

A multi-task transformer trained on 18 years of S&P 500 data, jointly predicting return, volatility, VaR₅, and CVaR₅ to test whether a post-hoc VaR calibration holds across market regimes. It doesn't: a frozen offset fails the Kupiec backtest in 4 of 5 walk-forward folds, and no static recalibration setting passes every regime. Shipped with MLflow experiment tracking and a versioned model registry, FastAPI serving with live drift monitoring, Docker, and CI. The research paper is currently in development for publication.
